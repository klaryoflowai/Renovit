## 1. STANDARDUL PRINCIPAL: EN 13279 SERIES

**EN 13279** = Gips si lianti gips pentru constructii (armonizat CPR 305/2011)

| **Partea** | **Titlu** | **Continut** |
|------------|-----------|-------------|
| **EN 13279-1:2008** | Definitii, specificatii, clasificare | **CLASE A, B, C** + cerinte minime |
| **EN 13279-2:2014** | Metode de testare | **8 teste obligatorii** pentru marcaj CE |

## 2. CERINTE CLASE GIPS - EN 13279-1:2008

### **CLASA A** (Premium - Placi gips-carton)
| Parametru | Cerinta minima |
|-----------|----------------|
| Timp inceput intarire | 45-90 minute |
| Rezistenta compresiune 2h | >= 5 MPa |
| Fineness (reziduu sita 0,2mm) | <= 12% |
| **Purity tipica** | **>= 95% CaSO4·2H2O** |

### **CLASA B** (Standard - **Tencuiala/glet**)
| Parametru | Cerinta minima |
|-----------|----------------|
| Timp inceput intarire | **10-45 minute** |
| Rezistenta compresiune 2h | **>= 2 MPa** |
| Fineness (reziduu sita 0,2mm) | **<= 15%** |
| **Purity tipica** | **90-95% CaSO4·2H2O** |

### **CLASA C** (Economic - Mortar interior)
| Parametru | Cerinta minima |
|-----------|----------------|
| Timp inceput intarire | 5-30 minute |
| Rezistenta compresiune 2h | >= 1 MPa |
| Fineness (reziduu sita 0,2mm) | <= 20% |

**TINTA EDINET: CLASA B** (tencuiala comerciala viabila)

## 3. DIFERENTE EN 13279-1 vs EN 13279-2

| **Aspect** | **EN 13279-1** | **EN 13279-2** |
|------------|----------------|----------------|
| **Scop** | **Specificatii + clasificare** | **Metode testare** |
| **Continut** | Clase A/B/C, cerinte minime | Proceduri laboratoare |
| **Utilizare** | **Declaratie performanta CE** | Certificare laboratoare acreditate |

## 4. MASURAREA CaSO4·2H2O - EN 13279-2:2014

### **Metoda oficiala: 5.2 Determinarea compozitiei chimice combinate**

```text
**Formula indirecta:** CaSO4·2H2O = [SO3(%) + CaO(%)] × Factor conversie

1. **Test SO3** (sulf trioxid):
   - Metoda gravimetrica (EN 196-2)
   - arzere + precipitare BaSO4

2. **Test CaO** (oxid calciu):
   - Complexometric (EDTA)
   - sau gravimetric

3. **Calcul:** Purity = (SO3 + 0,785 × CaO) × 1,93
```

**Rezultat asteptat Clasa B:** **>= 90% purity echivalenta**

## 5. TESTE SPECIFICE EN 13279-2 (8 TESTE OBLIGATORII)

| **Test nr.** | **Parametru** | **Metoda** | **Cerinta Clasa B** |
|--------------|---------------|------------|---------------------|
| **5.1** | Timp inceput intarire | Vicat | 10-45 min |
| **5.2** | **Compozitie chimica** | Gravimetrica | SO3 + CaO >=50% |
| **5.3** | Rezistenta compresiune 2h | Presiune hidraulica | **>= 2 MPa** |
| **5.4** | Expansiune in apa | Liniara | <= 30 mm |
| **5.5** | **Fineness** | Sit 0,2mm | **<= 15% reziduu** |
| **5.6** | Umiditate reziduala | Uscare 105°C | <= 0,5% |
| **5.7** | Densitate aparenta | HePyknometru | 900-1200 kg/m3 |
| **5.8** | Temperatura normalizare | 40°C/95% umiditate | Standardizare |

## 6. LIMITE IMPLICITE IMPURITATI (Practica industriala UE)

**EN 13279 NU limiteaza direct**, dar performanta impune:

| **Impuritate** | **Max Clasa B** | **Sursa limita** |
|----------------|-----------------|------------------|
| **Fe2O3** | **<= 0,5%** | Culoare (vizual) |
| **SiO2** | **<= 5%** | Rezistenta <2 MPa |
| **MgO** | **<= 3%** | Timp intarire >45 min |
| **Organice** | **<= 0,1%** | Miros/expansiune |
